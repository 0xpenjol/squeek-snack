WHAT IS SQUEEKCHAIN?

A blockchain powered by a real hamster. Yes, really.

🐹

A hamster runs the chain

Squeektoshi runs on his giant wheel, the WHEELY9000. Every step he takes is real work — measured live, right now.

🧱

Work becomes blocks

Every 10 minutes, a round settles: his wheel work turns into Squeek blocks. Nap rounds make nothing. Sprint rounds make many.

🪙

Blocks pay out SQK

Each block issues SQK, shared between everyone holding Squeek NFTs — the harder the hamster works, the more there is to share.

Squeekchain runs on the Sepolia testnet, so everything here is play money and test tokens — nothing on this site is worth real money, and no wallet is ever needed to play.

HOW TO PLAY

Snack Dash in three moves.

1

Move

Arrow keys, A / D, drag, or tap a side of the screen. The hamster follows you.

2

Catch

Coins are 10, berries 15, seeds 5. Three good catches in a row raise your combo — up to ×5. Missing one only drops it a level.

3

Survive

Cage bars cost a life. You have 3 lives and 60 seconds. Everything you catch goes to your inventory.

New: catch 🧲 magnet, 🛡️ shield and 🎡 2× wheel power-ups. Hit ×5 combo for 8 seconds of Fever (double points). When Squeektoshi finishes a round on the real wheel, a golden shower rains down.
